Updates the value in place as the running mean of the function.

The result is the running mean of the function over the part of the functional's own time window that has been simulated so far, normalised by the length of time actually sampled. It is therefore invariant to the length of the run: extending end_time of the simulation past the functional's window leaves the value unchanged, and a windowed functional reports the mean over its window rather than over the run.

◆ update_forcing_scale()

procedure, pass(this) objective::objective_t::update_forcing_scale ( class(objective_t), intent(inout) this,
type(time_state_t), intent(in) time,
real(kind=xp), intent(in) run_time )
inherited

The objective is the mean over its window, of length value_weight, and its forcing is the plain derivative of the integrand. The design coupling term is the mean over the whole run instead. The forcing is therefore scaled by the run length over the window length, and is off when the primal sample the step consumes lies outside the window. For an objective without a window the scale is exactly one.
