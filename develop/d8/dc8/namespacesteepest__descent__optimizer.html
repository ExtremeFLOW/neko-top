Detailed Description

Implements the steepest descent method with move limits (SDM) of Appendix D in da Silva, Aage, Beck & Sigmund, "Large scale three-dimensional manufacturing tolerant stress-constrained topology optimization", IJNME (2021), Eqs. D22-D24. Each iteration

  1. takes the steepest-descent direction S = -G and zeroes the components that point out of the box at an active bound (D22),
  2. normalises it by its global maximum absolute value,
  3. adapts the per-variable move limits from the sign of the last two design changes, from the third iteration on (D23),
  4. takes a move-limited, box-constrained step of length step_length (D24).

The method handles box bounds only; problems with constraints are rejected in favour of the "mma" optimizer. There is no tolerance-based convergence criterion: the step reports convergence only when the bound-projected gradient vanishes, and otherwise the run is stopped by the base criteria (max_iterations, stop_design_change, max_runtime).
