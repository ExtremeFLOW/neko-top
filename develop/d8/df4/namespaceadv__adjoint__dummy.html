type  adv_adjoint_dummy_t
 A zero-valued adjoint advection, used when the primal has no advection term (Stokes flow, case.fluid.advection = false). The linearised and adjoint convection terms are then identically zero, so every method is a no-op. Owns no memory, on the host or on the device. More...
