Detailed Description

Elementwise kernels of the steepest descent method with move limits, Appendix D of da Silva, Aage, Beck & Sigmund (2021), Eqs. D22-D24. Each routine operates on vector_t objects and runs on the device backend when one is configured, and on the CPU otherwise. All three are local operations; the global reduction of the direction is left to the caller.

Computes x_new = min(xmax, max(xmin, min(x + delta, max(x - delta, x + psi * (s / smax))))). This equals Eq. D24 whenever x lies in [xmin, xmax], and stays well defined for infeasible x.

[in]xCurrent design variables.
[in]sBound-projected steepest-descent direction.
[in]deltaMove limits.
[in]psiStep length.
[in]smaxGlobal maximum of the absolute value of s, which must be positive.
[in]xminLower bound of the design variables.
[in]xmaxUpper bound of the design variables.
[in]nNumber of entries to process, the size of x_new by default.

With d_e = (x_e - x1_e) * (x1_e - x2_e), the move limit is scaled by dec where d_e < 0, by inc where d_e > 0, left unchanged where d_e = 0, and then clamped to [dmin, dmax].
